# Introduction {#introduction}

Welcome\!

We’re thrilled to have you join us at Cal Hacks 13.0 this weekend\! All of us are super excited to kick off our thirteenth iteration of Cal Hacks, and you all are the driving force behind our event. We want to do everything we can to help you navigate Cal Hacks; with that said, please be sure to read through the entire guide\!

This year, Cal Hacks is taking place in San Francisco at the Palace of Fine Arts\! In this guide, you’ll find resources for navigating the venue and surrounding area, as well as an itinerary of the workshops, ceremonies, and events we’ll be hosting\!

Whether Cal Hacks is your first hackathon or your fiftieth, what we believe makes hackathons so valuable are the endless opportunities to learn. Cal Hacks wouldn’t be possible without the support from our community, from our incredible sponsors and mentors to our volunteers from across the nation. With their support, we’re excited to provide you with the resources you need to build out your projects from ideation to reality. We encourage you to check out our workshops, brought to you by our sponsors and Berkeley student organizations, and to reach out to our mentors — there’s no better way to discover new ways to build\!

What truly makes Cal Hacks special, though, is you. Each hacker brings a wealth of unique experiences and ideas; it’s your curiosity, creativity, and love for building that drives us to put on this event. Whether you come to San Francisco with a team or discover one when you arrive, the best ideas are formed from creative minds coming together to build something incredible. The projects that come out of Cal Hacks never fail to amaze; you inspire us every day to make Cal Hacks the best it can possibly be. Thank you so much for coming — we can’t wait to see what you build.

Happy hacking\!

Sincerely,  
The Hackathons @ Berkeley Team \<3

# Hacking {#hacking}

## **Registration & Team Formation** {#registration-&-team-formation}

- Every hacker must be registered to participate\!  
- Teams can be anywhere between **1-4 people**; no more than 4 people allowed under any circumstance.   
- No team? No problem\! Hop into the \#1-team-formation channel on Slack, **use the Team Matching feature in the app**, and pull up to our in-person team mixer event after the Opening Ceremony\!  
- Make sure to add your teammates on Devpost during submission. **No other confirmation is required to formally create a team.**

## **Project Submission & Judging** {#project-submission-&-judging}

All completed hacker projects must be submitted on **Devpost BY 9:00 AM,** on Sunday morning. ***Late projects will not be considered for judging, and you MUST be in-person at the venue for the judging period to be eligible for judging***. Shortly after the 9:00 AM submission deadline, all projects will be assigned a table number. You will need to move to your assigned table by 10:30 AM in order to be judged. Each table will be shared by 2-3 teams.

**All projects must be started during the event.** Sadly, we disqualify a number of entries every year for violating this rule. Your project should also follow the MLH [Code of Conduct](https://static.mlh.io/docs/mlh-code-of-conduct.pdf?_gl=1*1c6jcbt*_ga*MTAxODg5NjE0NC4xNjk4MjcyMTg2*_ga_E5KT6TC4TK*MTY5ODI3MjE4NS4xLjAuMTY5ODI3MjE4NS4wLjAuMA..).

**We highly recommend that you submit an initial draft of your project on Devpost by Saturday night, and continue editing your submission until the deadline, to avoid missing the deadline.**

Judges will visit project teams between **10:30am to 12:30pm**; this will be your chance to show off the amazing projects you’ve built\! **You must stay at your table for the full judging window\! If your entire team is not present while a judge comes, you will not be judged\!**  We recommend preparing a two-minute pitch and any answers to possible questions\! Hacks will be judged on a variety of factors, including impact, quality, technical complexity, and creativity. Our judges know that two days is a short timeframe and that many of our hackers are beginners, so don’t feel pressured to create the next BeReal — rather, we love to see projects that creatively attempt to tackle a problem, and care more about the idea, genuine effort, and thought process behind the project rather than the final product. That being said, **your pitch will require a live demo** in order to judge the actual functionality of your project.

Information about the prize tracks being offered can be found below\!

# FAQs {#faqs}

- **Do I need to show up for all 3 days?**  
  While the Cal Hacks experience is incomplete without attending the entirety of the event, you can arrive late or leave early if you have other commitments.  
    
- **I have a dietary restriction. Will Cal Hacks be able to accommodate?**   
  We will be offering vegetarian options as a part of our meals\! Due to the vast variety of dietary restrictions, however, we are unable to completely account for specific allergies or restrictions. For non-vegetarian hackers, we ask that you avoid grabbing vegetarian meals if possible to ensure vegetarian hackers are able to eat\! Please refer to our [food menu](https://tinyurl.com/ch-13-food) for detailed allergen information. If you have concerns about allergies/GF please reach out to a director and we can assist.  
    
- **Are hackers required to stay overnight?**  
  Nope\! Some of our hackers are returning to Berkeley for the night, or staying in different nearby hotels. Though staying overnight is a hackathon tradition, do whatever you feel most comfortable with\! Our sleeping areas will be divided into a men’s and women’s sleeping area to provide hackers more privacy.  
    
- **Will Ddoski (aka. huge teddy bear \+ our beloved mascot) be there?**   
  It wouldn’t be Cal Hacks without him. He’s grown a lot since you last saw him\!  
    
- **I lost something or found something someone lost, is there a lost and found area to retrieve or deposit it?**  
  Yes\! Come down to the front concierge desk where directors will be stationed to help you retrieve anything that has been deposited at the desk.   
    
- **Is there wifi at the event?**  
  Absolutely\! The wifi name is “Cal Hacks 13.0”, and the password is “hackers\!”
