###  Requirements 

  * Get to the venue by **October 25th @ 8 AM PST. **You must've had been accepted and checked in as a hacker to submit.
  * **You must submit your hack by October 25th @ 9 AM PST with your teammates!** This is a hard deadline.
  * Include an image of your project and GitHub repository for any code to be considered.
  * All work must be done during the hackathon
  * You will have three minutes to pitch and two minutes for judges to ask questions.
  * **You must have a pre-recorded or live demonstration.**

###  Judging Criteria 

  * **Application**  
Projects should have a feasible real-world use. Could someone realistically adopt this, and would it deliver a clear benefit? Strong submissions identify who they help, what problem they solve, and why people would actually use them. 
  * **Intentionality**  
Projects should offer an intuitive, engaging experience for their target users, guided by clear first principles. Every design choice should feel purposeful, with a clear sense of who it's for and why it works the way it does. 
  * **Functionality**  
Projects should work as intended, with a live demo that runs smoothly free of major bugs. Submissions should also include a clear, meaningful video demo on devpost for sponsors evaluating asynchronously. 
  * **Creativity**  
Projects should show original thinking and a fresh perspective, with inventive problem framing and unexpected combinations of ideas or technologies. Clearly show what makes your project stand out from similar work. 
  * **Technical Complexity**  
Teams should show a command of the frameworks and infrastructure behind their project, with sound architectural choices and engineering depth that goes beyond basic implementation.
