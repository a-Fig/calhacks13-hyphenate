Venue, Transportation, & Accommodations
---------------------------------------

🌃 *Venue*
Cal Hacks 13.0 will take place from October 23rd to 25th at the Palace of Fine Arts, located at 3601 Lyon St, San Francisco, CA 94123. You can use this Google Maps link ( https://maps.app.goo.gl/FJkb4qBnWp7Snw6x8 ) to navigate to the front entrance. Doors will open at 1:30 PM on Friday and we’ll conclude by 4 PM on Sunday.

🚐 *Transportation*
The Palace of Fine Arts is located in San Francisco's Marina District. We'll be offering a free hacker bus from the BART line to the venue for accepted and confirmed hackers, with more details to come! The venue is also reachable by Muni bus lines 28, 30, 43, and more. Parking is limited, so we recommend taking public transit or rideshare.

🏨 *Accommodations*
Food, beverages, and swag are provided to all hackers free of charge. We'll have a sleeping area at the venue if you want to get some rest, so feel free to bring a sleeping bag! There are also many local hotels within a 10-minute walk should you opt to book one. We will not be offering hotel or lodging reimbursements.

Food, beverages, swag, tons of API credits, and of course energy drinks will be provided to all hackers free of charge. Plenty of prizes will be awarded to the winning teams (check out the list of tracks on our Hacker Guide!).

Meet Some (more) of Our Sponsors!
---------------------------------

Our sponsors will be here all weekend to support you with credits, swag, and mentors for your project. Think of the hackathon like a large hands-on career fair and showcase to show off your skills! Many of the sponsors are here to recruit from the hacker pool, and many participants in the past have walked away with interviews and even jobs. Please take a moment before the hackathon to review the sponsors and come prepared with your questions and ideas.

*Anthropic (Co-Host)* — Anthropic is an AI safety and research company, and the team behind Claude. At Cal Hacks 13.0, our track is 'Claude for Public Benefit': pick a community (people who share a place, a circumstance, or a need), find a real problem they face, and build something with Claude that helps. We’re also hosting a Saturday workshop on building agents and multi-agent teams, and holding coffee chats in our Claude Corner about careers, research, and building with Claude. Come find our team at the Anthropic booth!

*Annapurna Labs (Co-Host)* — At Annapurna Labs (an Amazon company), we design the custom silicon that powers the modern cloud. Our chips sit at the heart of AWS, running some of the largest workloads in the world. And we don't just design the chips; we build the full stack around them, from the chip architecture itself all the way up to the firmware and software that bring it to life.

*Spacetime (Co-Host)* — Spacetime is excited to be an Anchor sponsor of CalHacks 13.0. Spacetime combines your database, backend logic, and real-time sync in one platform so you can spend the weekend building your idea instead of stitching together infrastructure. Stop by our booth for hands-on help and compete in our "Best Use of Spacetime" track for $2,048 for first place, $1,024 for second, and $512 for third. We’re also hosting a separate Scrabblebot tournament, where you’ll create a bot that bids on Scrabble letters and competes against other bots, with 1st place $1,024 and 2nd place and $512 and 3rd place $256 going to the winner!

*Greptile* — Greptile is an AI code review platform that reviews and tests pull requests with complete context of the codebase. It catches bugs that other tools miss by building a graph of your repo and deploying a swarm of agents to review each PR with full context. It learns your team's coding standards over time from engineer comments, reactions, and custom rules. We're hiring across the board for internships and full time!

*Atlassian* — Atlassian builds collaboration and productivity tools, including Jira, Confluence, and Loom, to help millions of teams around the world unleash their full potential. We’re excited to offer an Agentic Challenge track where you can build innovative AI-driven solutions for a chance to win Apple AirPods Max. To get inspired and learn more about how we’re shaping the future of work with AI, check out https://www.atlassian.com/blog ( https://www.atlassian.com/blog ).

*Context.dev* — Context.dev gives your agents and apps access to web search, scraping, and people and company data. In our workshop, you’ll learn how to build an agent that can go through hundreds of thousands of pages to find deals on items you're interested in!

*Wajo* — Wajo builds Fo, an AI action agent that gets real-world tasks done for you, from booking appointments to coordinating with people by phone and email. With Fo MCP, you can bring Fo into the AI tools and projects you already use.

*Mana* — Mana is the fastest way to build your project. Whether you build inside Mana or in your own stack on GitHub, it helps you quickly visualize ideas, test prototypes and build the real version. Build together inside Mana with no setup, API keys or deploys. All teams using Mana get 3 months of Pro (equivalent to Claude Max), and the best project wins $5,000.

*Bright Data* — Bright Data is the world’s leading public web data platform, helping developers build AI apps, agents, and insights powered by public web data. We’re excited to share our Builder-in-Residence program, a 3-month virtual residency with a program stipend and mentorship for students building open-source AI/data projects with Bright Data’s web infrastructure. Join our workshop to power your hack with public web data and compete in our “Best Agent/App Using Public Web Data” prize track, with Amazon prizes and Bright Data credits for the top 3 teams.
