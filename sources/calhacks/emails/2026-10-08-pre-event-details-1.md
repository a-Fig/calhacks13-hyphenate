DDoSki’s Hack Month
-------------------

We’re launching the month of Cal Hacks 13.0 with DDoSki’s Hack Month! This is a month-long series of workshops and events leading up to the event, intended for you to meet other hackers, think of crazy ideas, learn new skills, and find potential teammates. We’ve already kicked off DDoSki’s Hack Month with our Women’s Hack Night on 10/2, and we have many more events to follow. RSVP for all of the events at the link below!

Venue, Transportation, & Accommodations
---------------------------------------

*🌃 Venue*
Cal Hacks 13.0 will take place from October 23rd to 25th at the Palace of Fine Arts, located at 3601 Lyon St, San Francisco, CA 94123. You can use this Google Maps link ( https://maps.app.goo.gl/FJkb4qBnWp7Snw6x8 ) to navigate to the front entrance. Doors will open at 1:30 PM on Friday and we’ll conclude by 4 PM on Sunday.

*🚐 Transportation*
The Palace of Fine Arts is located in San Francisco's Marina District. We'll be offering a free hacker bus from the BART line to the venue for accepted and confirmed hackers, with more details to come! The venue is also reachable by Muni bus lines 28, 30, 43, and more. Parking is limited, so we recommend taking public transit or rideshare.

*🏨 Accommodations*
Food, beverages, and swag are provided to all hackers free of charge. We'll have a sleeping area at the venue if you want to get some rest, so feel free to bring a sleeping bag! There are also many local hotels within a 10-minute walk should you opt to book one. We will not be offering hotel or lodging reimbursements.

Food, beverages, swag, tons of API credits, and of course energy drinks will be provided to all hackers free of charge. Plenty of prizes will be awarded to the winning teams (stay tuned for more info in our Hacker Guide!).

Packing List
------------

We recommend you bring...

* Government-issued ID with DOB (required)
* Laptop & charger
* Power bank
* Headphones
* Reusable water bottle
* Sleeping bag / air mattress (we will have separate sleeping areas for male and female identifying hackers)
* Towel + body wash (we will have showers open during select times)
* Toiletries, deodorant, change of clothes, personal hygiene items

----------------
Prohibited Items
----------------

Please note that the following items are NOT allowed at the venue and are subject to confiscation, removal from the building, permanent blacklist from our events, and/or escalation to law enforcement.

* No open flames or flammable items, including but not limited to gasoline, gas torches, lighter fluid, etc.
* No weapons (guns, knives, pocket knives, fireworks, improvised weapons, etc.)
* No items that pose a danger to you or others
* No illegal drugs, alcoholic beverages, or tobacco products (including e-cigarettes)
* No tents
* No animals
* No routers or equipment that interfere with wireless infrastructure
