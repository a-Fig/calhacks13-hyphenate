# Hyphenate (formerly Maximor)

The autonomous finance platform. One context. Agents that do the work.

## About us

Hyphenate is the autonomous finance platform that runs on top of the ERP and systems finance already uses. It connects them in one shared context and learns how finance works from the systems, spreadsheets, and workflows already in place. Teams don’t have to document every process from scratch or write prompts and rules to put agents to work.

Agents do 98% of the finance work they touch. Every action shows its work and is fully auditable. Books reconcile continuously, so the numbers are always current, not just after month-end. The platform gets sharper with every escalation, every judgment call, every close.
