## What moved, and why.

### One ledger, any chart of accounts for reporting

Entities keep posting to their own chart of accounts. Hyphenate maps it to whatever chart your reporting needs: a sponsor's, a parent company's, or a lender's.

### Nothing unmapped slips through

New or reclassed accounts are flagged until mapped, so gaps surface before the numbers go out, not after a sponsor or the board asks a question.

### Ask, and get the analysis

Type a plain-English question into Hyphenate and get the financial analysis back, no manual pivot tables required.

### Flux that explains the why, not just the what

Variance commentary is AI-generated and drills to the transactions behind it, pulling the real driver from a CRM deal, a contract, or a vendor bill, not just the GL.

### Board-ready, on demand

Board decks and historical comparisons are generated straight from the connected data. One system behind every report, deck, and explanation.

### Gets smarter every close

Every correction a reviewer makes becomes a precedent the system reasons from next time, so accuracy climbs period over period.
