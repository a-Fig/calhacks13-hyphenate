## **About Hyphenate**

**Hyphenate** (formerly [Maximor.ai](http://Maximor.ai)) is a New York-based startup, founded in 2023 by former Microsoft executives, building AI agents that automate finance and accounting workflows for mid-market and enterprise companies. The platform connects directly to ERP, CRM, billing, payroll, and banking systems to handle tasks like revenue recognition, accounts payable, and reconciliation — replacing the manual, Excel-driven work that has traditionally bogged down finance teams during month-end close. Hyphenate has raised $9M in seed funding led by Foundation Capital, with participation from Gaia Ventures, Boldcap, and others.

## **About the Role**

Hyphenate is hiring an India-based Technical Recruiter to own outbound sourcing and closing for U.S. software engineering and technical roles. This is a hands-on, outbound-first sourcing role for someone who has already closed engineering hires for a U.S. product company and is comfortable running cold outreach at volume.

## **Work Schedule & Hours**

This is a remote role based in India that supports a U.S. hiring team. Please note the following before applying:

  * **6-day work week.** The role operates on a six-day schedule (Monday through Saturday).

  * **Late India-time hours.** Working hours are shifted later in the day (IST) to provide meaningful overlap with U.S. business hours for candidate calls, hiring-team syncs, and scheduling.

  * **Flexibility on both sides.** Exact hours will be agreed with the Head of Talent and may flex around candidate availability across U.S. time zones.

## **Key Responsibilities**

  * Own end-to-end sourcing and outbound recruitment for U.S.-based software engineering and technical roles.

  * Design and run outbound campaigns — cold email sequences and cold calling — to build qualified candidate pipelines.

  * Use AI-enabled sourcing tools to identify, enrich, and prioritize candidate lists.

  * Manage candidate communication end-to-end: outreach, screening, scheduling, and pipeline updates.

  * Partner closely with the hiring team to refine sourcing criteria based on candidate quality and market feedback.

## **Required Experience**

  * 2–4 years of full-cycle recruiting experience at a fast-growing, U.S.-based startup (seed through Series B).

  * Startup experience must be from a product company — SaaS, AI agents, or enterprise software. E-commerce and non-product companies do not qualify.

  * Track record of having sourced and closed multiple U.S.-based software engineering roles — this is a minimum bar, not a nice-to-have.

  * Strong, demonstrated experience with outbound recruitment: cold email campaigns and cold calling. Inbound recruiting experience is a plus but not required.

  * Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.

  * Comfortable using AI tools as part of the day-to-day sourcing workflow.

  * Able to commit to a 6-day work week with late India-time hours aligned to U.S. business hours.

  * Consistent, stable work history — frequent unexplained employment gaps will not meet the bar.

## **Preferred / Nice to Have**

  * Familiarity with modern AI-powered sourcing platforms such as Juicebox or MetaView.

  * Experience with sourcing/outreach tools commonly used for U.S. technical hiring, such as LinkedIn Recruiter, Gem, hireEZ (formerly Hiretual), SeekOut, GitHub (with tools like Octohunt), and Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent).

## **Compensation & Benefits**

  * **Very competitive pay.** Compensation benchmarked well above market for the role and experience level.

  * **Performance-based bonus.** Additional earnings tied to hiring outcomes and pipeline quality.

## **Hiring Process**

  * **Round 1 — Screening:** Introductory conversation with the Hyphenate Head of Talent.

  * **Round 2 — Task:** A short take-home exercise (a few hours) to assess sourcing approach and output.

  * **Round 3 — Paid Work Trial:** Successful candidates move to a one-week, paid work trial using Hyphenate's own tools and live requisitions.

  * **Final Round:** A closing conversation with Hyphenate leadership.
