**About Hyphenate**

Most AI companies in finance are building copilots. Hyphenate builds AI agents that do the work.

Hyphenate is the autonomous finance platform for the office of the CFO. Our agents run on top of a company's existing ERP, banks, billing, CRM and contracts, and take on the work itself: order-to-cash and revenue recognition, cash and treasury, procure-to-pay, general ledger and close, and reporting. They learn how each company runs finance, log every action for auditors, and escalate the decisions that need human judgment. No ERP migration, no rip-and-replace.

We are based in New York City and backed by a $9M seed round led by Foundation Capital, with BoldCap, Gaia Ventures, Aravind Srinivas (CEO of Perplexity) and finance leaders from Zuora, Ramp, Gusto, MongoDB, Zoom and the Big Four. Customers include Kiteworks, HiBid, Dura Software, Mini Melts and Rently. The average customer runs six modules, and 40% expand within their first contract year.

**About the Role**

We are hiring a Private Equity Partnerships Lead to build our PE channel from zero to one.

Private equity has become our strongest pull. PE-backed companies are over-represented in our customer base, and the same pattern keeps repeating: we deploy at one portfolio company, the operating partner hears about it, and they come to us asking how to roll Hyphenate out across the portfolio. So far that has happened organically, with the channel shared between our CRO, CEO and Chief of Staff. This role exists so that one person owns it and makes it happen on purpose.

This is a true business development role, not a closing role. You will own our relationships with PE firms, especially operating partners and portfolio value-creation teams, and turn them into a repeatable source of warm, qualified pipeline for our Account Executives. You are measured on closed ARR that you sourced.

You start as an individual contributor and the first hire on this channel. The mandate is to bring the relationships, build the playbook, and prove it works before we add a team around it. You report to the Chief Revenue Officer, who has launched and run partnerships at FloQast and Trovata, and you will work directly with our CEO, who has personally driven much of our PE traction to date.

**What You'll Own**

  * Build the PE channel from 0 to 1: the target firm list, the outreach motion, the partner value proposition, and the handoff into sales

  * Open and own relationships with operating partners, portfolio operations, value creation and finance leaders at the top 50 to 100 PE firms investing in our segments: B2B software, CPG, manufacturing, wholesale distribution, and multi-entity roll-ups

  * Turn firm relationships into pipeline: map each sponsor's portfolio, identify the companies with the finance complexity we solve, and source warm introductions to their CFOs and Controllers

  * Run the partner funnel and report on it weekly: firms engaged, portfolio companies introduced, meetings sourced, and closed ARR

  * Design and test an operating-partner motion: packaged multi-portco deployments that a sponsor can prioritize and roll out across its portfolio

  * Convert customer wins into sponsor relationships: work with our customer and solutions teams to turn a happy portfolio company into a portfolio-wide conversation

  * Be present in the PE ecosystem: operating partner forums, events, dinners, and the advisory and consulting firms that sit around PE

  * Partner with Account Executives on every sourced deal from intro through close, and with Marketing on PE-specific content, case studies and events

  * Bring the market back inside: which firms convert, what operating partners care about, and what the product needs for portfolio-wide deployment
