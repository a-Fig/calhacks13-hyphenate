Features & benefits

## What happens after the cash lands.

### Multi-bank, multi-entity, multi-currency feeds

Every bank account, in every entity and currency, flows into one working view of cash.

### An AI agent that triages every transaction

Every bank line is checked against your ERP, open invoices and bills, then reconciled or categorized automatically. Only real exceptions reach a human.

### Confidence-scored, human-approved

Every suggestion carries a confidence score and its evidence, and nothing posts to the ERP without a click, so review time goes to real exceptions.

### Native FX and batch-payment handling

Foreign-currency rates resolve automatically wherever the data supports it, and a single bank debit that clears many bills reconciles as one group.

### Automation bank reconciliation statement

A full BRS is prepared for every account every period, with variances surfaced daily instead of at month-end, exportable to Excel.

### A cash forecast that's always current

A rolling 13-week forecast updates automatically as new transactions clear, so nobody maintains a separate model by hand.
