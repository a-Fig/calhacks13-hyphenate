Features & benefits

## What used to live in a spreadsheet.

### AI-native subledgers, not spreadsheets with a UI

Fixed assets, prepaids, R&D cap, lease, and intercompany each learn your policies from history and do the work, with a person approving, not authoring.

### Fixed assets that build their own register

New assets are detected automatically from bills in your AP stack, tested against your capitalization policy, and reconciled against your IT asset tracker.

### Prepaids on autopilot

Amortization schedules build themselves the moment a bill is coded, and roll forward to the GL with proven zero variance, across books and currencies.

### Lease accounting, fully touchless

An AI agent reads the lease schedule, applies ASC 842 classification, and prepares the monthly entry itself, running inside the close checklist.

### A close checklist that executes tasks

Preparer and reviewer workflows work as expected, but checklist rows can trigger an AI agent to actually run the task, from the row itself.

### Reconciliations that pull their own balances

Tag a cell in your workpaper and Hyphenate reads the balance directly, while every item is auto-classified and aged for exposure.
