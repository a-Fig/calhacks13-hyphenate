Hyphenate (prev Maximor) is AI-native finance automation software purpose built for accounting & finance teams. It's built-in AI agents eliminate manual spreadsheet busywork across core accounting, month-end book close, and financial MIS reporting workflows – enabling teams to focus on strategic activities while simplifying compliance and strengthening control rigor.

Hyphenate integrates securely with a company’s existing systems without requiring system migrations or complex implementation projects, providing automation and audit-ready accuracy on top of the current finance stack.
