# Hyphenate Raises $9M Seed to Bring Trustworthy AI to Finance Without Rip-and-Replace

Hyphenate closes a $9M seed round led by Foundation Capital to build audit-ready AI agents that layer onto your existing ERP. No migration required.

Team Hyphenate

Because finance doesn’t need another ERP – it needs an AI teammate that works well with existing systems

Today, we’re excited to share that **Hyphenate has raised $9M in seed funding** , led by **Foundation Capital** , with participation from BoldCap, Gaia Ventures, Boldcap, and a group of incredible founders, CFOs and finance leaders from companies like Perplexity, Zuora, Zoom, Ramp, Gusto MongoDB, and the Big 4.

Funding is a milestone — but for us, it’s only fuel for what comes next: building the first **AI-powered finance team** that quietly takes on the back-end grind.

## Why Finance Is Still Broken

Every month, finance teams spend nights and weekends pulling data from fragmented systems, reconciling accounts, parsing contracts, and building reports — all by hand. ERP migrations promise transformation but still leave teams stuck in spreadsheets, chasing the close.

Finance leaders don’t want disruption. They want business-as-usual, just without the busywork.

## What Hyphenate Does

**Hyphenate is an AI agent platform for finance.**

Our agents connect to the systems you already use — ERPs, CRMs, billing platforms, payroll, banks — and automate the manual work:

  * Reconciling accounts and transactions
  * Preparing and posting journal entries
  * Parsing contracts and allocating revenue
  * Managing close checklists and flux analysis
  * Delivering audit-ready report packages

Instead of spending days assembling numbers, AI does the prep. Finance teams review only the exceptions, with every output fully traceable and audit-ready.

## Why Now

The timing couldn’t be more urgent:

  * **75% of accountants are nearing retirement** while the CPA pipeline has shrunk by 30% in the last decade.
  * **Finance complexity is exploding** with multi-entity operations, usage-based pricing, and increased audit scrutiny.
  * Adding headcount isn’t **sustainable** — but automation that scales with finance leaders is.

That’s where Hyphenate’s AI agents come in.

## Built Different

We designed Hyphenate with three principles in mind:

  * **System-agnostic** : Works across ERPs, banks, CRMs, billing, and payroll — no migrations needed.
  * **Audit-ready outputs** : Every reconciliation, journal entry, and report is fully traceable and reviewable.
  * **Human + AI** : Agents do the heavy lifting of prep; finance teams provide oversight and strategy.

The result: a finance system that feels less like another SaaS add-on and more like a trusted teammate who never sleeps.

## What’s Next

With this funding, we’re doubling down on product development across key controllership workflows. We’re expanding our engineering and AI research teams, and partnering with more finance leaders to prove that finance doesn’t need to be trapped in busywork.
