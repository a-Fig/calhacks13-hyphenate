# Hyphenate, the company

Short summary. Full text is in the linked sources.

## Who they are

- "The autonomous finance platform for the office of the CFO" ([home](../sources/hyphenate/site/home.md)). To hackers: "AI agents that run the books for real companies" ([email](../sources/calhacks/emails/2026-10-16-meet-hyphenate.md)).
- Formerly Maximor. Renamed Oct 1, 2026, citing 86x revenue growth, a company-reported figure ([their press page](../sources/hyphenate/site/press-maximor-rebrand-hyphenate.md), [GlobeNewswire](../sources/hyphenate/press/globenewswire-2026-10-01-rebrand.md), [TNW](../sources/hyphenate/press/tnw-2026-10-01-rebrand.md)).
- Founded 2024 by Ramnandan Krishnamurthy (CEO) and Ajay Krishna Amudan (CTO), both ex-Microsoft. HQ New York, office in Bengaluru ([TechCrunch](../sources/hyphenate/press/techcrunch-2025-09-29-seed.md), [AlleyWatch](../sources/hyphenate/press/alleywatch-2025-09-30-funding-report.md)). LinkedIn lists 51-200 employees ([LinkedIn](../sources/hyphenate/linkedin-about.md)).
- $9M seed led by Foundation Capital, Sep 2025, with Gaia Ventures, BoldCap and finance-leader angels ([GlobeNewswire](../sources/hyphenate/press/globenewswire-2025-09-29-seed.md), [Foundation Capital](../sources/hyphenate/press/foundationcapital-2025-09-29-investment.md), [their blog](../sources/hyphenate/site/blog/maximor-raises-9m-to-give-cfos-audit-ready-finance-automation-without-erp-rip-and-replace.md), [CFO Dive](../sources/hyphenate/press/cfodive-2025-seed.md)). They told hackers "We just raised a big Series A" ([email](../sources/calhacks/emails/2026-10-16-meet-hyphenate.md)). No public Series A announcement found.
- Anchor sponsor of Cal Hacks 13.0 with Anthropic, Annapurna Labs and SpacetimeDB ([devpost-overview.md](../sources/calhacks/devpost-overview.md)).
- Hiring. 15 open roles, mostly on-site in New York, including Software Engineer (New Grad) and Software Engineering Intern ([careers](../sources/hyphenate/site/careers/index.md)). Applications through Cal Hacks are prioritized ([email](../sources/calhacks/emails/2026-10-16-meet-hyphenate.md)).

## What the product does

- Runs on top of existing systems with no rip-and-replace. "Your ledger stays the system of record" ([platform](../sources/hyphenate/site/platform.md)).
- Learns a company's policies by watching how it runs, agents do the work, escalates to the right person, audit trail on every entry ([home](../sources/hyphenate/site/home.md), [platform](../sources/hyphenate/site/platform.md)).
- Five product areas: [order-to-cash](../sources/hyphenate/site/order-to-cash.md), [treasury](../sources/hyphenate/site/treasury.md), [GL accounting](../sources/hyphenate/site/gl-accounting.md), [procure-to-pay](../sources/hyphenate/site/procure-to-pay.md), [reporting](../sources/hyphenate/site/reporting.md). Role pages for the [CFO](../sources/hyphenate/site/cfo.md) and [controller](../sources/hyphenate/site/controller.md).
- Customers named include Kiteworks, HiBid, Dura Software and Mini Melts USA ([home](../sources/hyphenate/site/home.md), case studies [Kiteworks](../sources/hyphenate/site/case-study-kiteworks.md) and [HiBid](../sources/hyphenate/site/case-study-hibid.md)). Marketing claims: "90% of routine work runs on its own" ([home](../sources/hyphenate/site/home.md)), agents "do 98% of the finance work they touch" ([LinkedIn](../sources/hyphenate/linkedin-about.md)).
- SOC 2 Type 2 ([trust center](../sources/hyphenate/site/trust-center.md)).

## Why it matters for the pitch

- The judging questions mirror their product: agents that do the work, escalate, keep an audit trail and trace numbers to the source ([platform](../sources/hyphenate/site/platform.md), [track brief](../sources/hyphenate/track-brief.md)).
- Build on top of a ledger and other sources rather than replacing them, the way they do.
- Their five product areas line up with the brief's sample workflows, so a corner they don't cover is "something new".
