# Hyphenate, the company

Checked on hyphenate.ai and press coverage on Oct 23, 2026.

## Who they are

- Hyphenate (hyphenate.ai) builds "the autonomous finance platform for the office of the CFO". Their Cal Hacks intro puts it as "AI agents that run the books for real companies".
- Formerly Maximor. The rename was announced Oct 1, 2026 ("Maximor Rebrands as Hyphenate After 86x YoY Growth, Expanding Across the Office of the CFO"). The 86x revenue growth figure is company-reported.
- Co-founded in summer 2024 by Ramnandan Krishnamurthy (CEO) and Ajay Krishna Amudan (CTO), both formerly at Microsoft (TechCrunch, Sep 2025).
- Headquartered in New York with an office in Bengaluru (TechCrunch, Sep 2025). Their careers section lists roles on-site in New York.
- Raised a $9M seed round led by Foundation Capital in Sep 2025, with Gaia Ventures, BoldCap and finance-leader angels. Their Cal Hacks intro says "We just raised a big Series A". The Series A amount and investors are unverified, and I found no public announcement of it.
- Anchor sponsor and one of the four presenting sponsors of Cal Hacks 13.0 (with Anthropic, Annapurna Labs and SpacetimeDB).
- Hiring. The Cal Hacks intro links a New Grad Software Engineer (2027) role and a Software Engineering Intern (Summer 2027 or earlier) role, and says applications through Cal Hacks are prioritized.

## What the product does

Their framing, from hyphenate.ai.

- Runs on top of the systems a company already has (ERP and the tools around it), with no rip-and-replace. "Your ledger stays the system of record. Agents read from it, post approved entries, and connect everything in between."
- Learns a company's policies and workflows "by watching how your business runs", and every exception, judgment call and escalation feeds back in.
- Agents do the work, and when judgment is needed the platform escalates and brings in the right person.
- Audit-ready by default. Every step is documented as it happens and every entry carries its own audit trail.

Five product areas.

| Area | What their site says it does |
|---|---|
| Order-to-cash | Reads signed contracts, applies the company's ASC 606 policy, builds the revenue waterfall, sends invoices and chases payment |
| Treasury | Matches bank activity to open bills and receivables, flags short-paid or unmatched items, reconciles to the bank daily, 13-week cash forecast |
| GL accounting | Runs the close checklist, subledgers (fixed assets, prepaids, leases, R&D capitalization), posts reconciled entries, drafts flux commentary |
| Procure-to-pay | Reads and codes vendor bills in any format, splits allocations, proposes accruals, routes approvals, chases vendors |
| Reporting and insights | Rolls every entity and ERP into one picture, builds board decks, answers questions in plain language traced to the entry behind them |

Integrations named in TechCrunch (Sep 2025, as Maximor): ERPs like NetSuite and Intacct, accounting tools like QuickBooks and Zoho Books, and payroll, CRM and other SaaS tools. A customer quote on their site describes agents layered onto SAP at Mini Melts USA.

Customers named on their site include Kiteworks, HiBid, Dura Software and Mini Melts USA. Their homepage claims "90% of routine work runs on its own" and, in its page description, that agents "do 98% of the finance work they touch". These are their marketing numbers.

## Why it matters for the pitch

- The track is their product thesis in miniature. Their site sells exactly what the judging asks for: agents that do the work, escalate to the right person, keep an audit trail, and trace every number back to its source. Showing those four things maps straight onto "Would you hire it?".
- They stress running on top of a company's existing systems, with the ledger as the system of record. A build where our system acts on a ledger and other sources, rather than replacing them, matches how they think.
- Their five product areas line up with the brief's sample workflows. A company or corner of finance they haven't seen made autonomous is what "Did we see something new?" asks for.
- They are hiring engineers and give top-5 teams an expedited interview process.
