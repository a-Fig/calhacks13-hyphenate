# What already connects PLM, ERP and finance for engineering changes

Researched 2026-10-24 by a Sonnet research agent.

**Legend.** **R** = I read the page. **S** = only a search-result summary (page blocked or not opened). **V** = vendor's own claim, no independent check. **Inf** = my inference. Where a page returned HTTP 403 I say so. Dates are the page's date, or the date I fetched if none showed.

## Bottom line

1. **The glue exists, and it is dumb on purpose.** PLM-ERP connectors move records: part/material, BOM, routing, documents, and the change itself as a Change Master. I found none that moves a cost estimate, a lead-time forecast or an impact judgement. Our product should not be built or pitched as glue.
2. **The real competition is analysis moving inside the PLM, not glue.** Siemens shipped an "AI BOM agent" in Teamcenter (blog 2026-06-23) that analyses impact on BOMs inside governed workflows. Aras told analysts in April 2026 that it is building toward predicting design-change impacts "across costs, schedules, and margins" (roadmap, not shipped). Siemens' Intelligence Center X is pitched as agents over PLM, ERP and CRM data. Of these, only the Siemens BOM agent is shipped, and its page says nothing about cost or ERP.
3. **I found no product that does what we do end to end**: an autonomous, cross-functional impact assessment with ranges that lands as a statement on the ECR and as cost effects in the ERP for finance. My search tools were limited (many vendor pages returned 403, and I could not query funding databases). A stealth startup, or a Palantir build inside Archer, is plausible and I could not rule it out.
4. **Evidence for "the human glue" is weaker than vendors imply.** Almost every claim that ECRs run on Excel and email comes from PLM vendors selling the cure. Independent studies are old and small. I found no data on CCB prep effort.
5. **Recommendation:** integrate directly with the PLM and ERP through their own APIs and keep the adapters thin and replaceable. Treat an iPaaS (Workato, at Archer) as an optional transport, not the architecture. Position as "the analyst the connectors don't have", sold on the judgement and its audit trail.

## 2. iPaaS: Workato, MuleSoft, Boomi, SAP Integration Suite

- **Workato.** Its SAP connector is the generic RFC/IDoc one (S: [docs](https://docs.workato.com/connectors/sap/sap-connector)). I fetched Workato's [connector list](https://docs.workato.com/en/connectors/prebuilt-connectors) and found Propel but no Teamcenter, Windchill, Arena, Aras or ENOVIA. I searched for a Workato ECO, BOM or PLM recipe and found none. So an Archer recipe for engineering change would be custom-built on a generic connector. That is an absence of evidence in public listings, not proof.
- **MuleSoft.** I searched for Teamcenter and Windchill connectors in Anypoint Exchange: nothing found (S). MuleSoft's docs say that without a prebuilt connector you build a REST/SOAP one.
- **Boomi.** Only a GetApp comparison page mentions BOM transfer with ECO revisions, with no Boomi documentation behind it (S, V).
- **SAP BTP Integration Suite.** SAP markets PLM integration (above). I found no named Integration Suite package for Teamcenter or Windchill change; the Business Accelerator Hub was not reachable.
- **Who fills the gap.** Specialist integrators and PLM vendors, not iPaaS vendors: PROSTEP, TT PSC, Technia, xPLM, Innoface (CoreFace for Windchill, listed on [SAP's site](https://www.sap.com/uk/products/technology-platform/partners/innoface-ag-coreface-for-windchill.html), S). PTC launched **Arena Connect** on 2026-08-04: 200+ connectors and templates for Jira, Teams and Slack, with ECOs kept in sync with Jira ([release via Webull](https://www.webull.com/news/15344576942269440), R, V). PLM vendors are building their own iPaaS.

Not found: any figure for CCB preparation effort, or for how many ECRs get an actual cost estimate. Finance-side practice is described only in vendor glossaries: finance should assess standard cost, purchase commitments, inventory valuation and WIP when an ECN changes the economics ([Hyperbots](https://www.hyperbots.com/glossary/engineering-change-management), S). That page does not say who owns the standard cost update, and I found no source linking ECOs to purchase price variance.

**What the human glue likely is (Inf).** The PLM and ERP hold the records. The impact judgement is assembled by a configuration manager and function reps from the PLM's affected-items view, an ERP inventory and PO lookup, supplier emails and a cost engineer's spreadsheet, and it is discussed at the CCB. I could not verify this for Archer or any aerospace firm, and no source I found describes Slack as a channel for it. Treat the claim as a hypothesis to check with the Archer staff you already talk to.

## 5. Where the gap is

| Capability | Exists today | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| ECO/BOM/material sync PLM to ERP | Yes, mature (Teamcenter T4S, Windchill ESI, Technia, PROSTEP, xPLM) | Section 1 |
| Where-used / affected-item impact in the PLM | Yes, and AI-assisted since June 2026 (Siemens) | Section 3 |
| Part-level should-cost from CAD | Yes (aPriori, TcPCM, SEER) | Section 3, mostly V |
| Supplier-network risk, what-if scenarios | Yes, separate products (Resilinc, Interos) | Section 3 |
| ECO cost/timing simulation against inventory and orders | Inside some ERPs (Glovia), concept in SAP | Section 3 |
| **One autonomous assessment that combines cost range, effectivity timing, supply risk and purchase price variance, writes it onto the ECR with an audit trail of assumptions, and passes cost effects to finance** | **Not found as a shipped product** | Searches listed in sections 2-3 |

What is partly built elsewhere: Siemens (impact), Aras (stated roadmap for cost, schedule, margin), Makersite (BOM-level cost and risk data in Teamcenter), Palantir-style custom builds. What I did not find anywhere: output as **ranges with traceable assumptions**, **PPV and standard-cost effects aimed at finance**, or a **record that finance can audit**. The strongest case against us is that Siemens and Aras are heading here and own the PLM data. Our edge (Inf) is that the answer needs ERP, supplier and volume-plan data that the PLM does not own, and a neutral view across tools.

## 6. Recommendation

**Integrate directly with PLM and ERP; optionally ride an iPaaS.**
- **Direct, thin adapters** for: trigger on ECR creation or state change; read the affected BOM, standard cost, inventory, open POs and supplier terms; write back the impact statement to the ECR; write cost effects to the ERP as proposals. No iPaaS vendor offers a PLM-ECO-to-ERP-cost recipe, so one adds a hop without adding logic.
- **iPaaS as transport only.** Archer's postings name Workato (see `archer-public-data.md`). Offering "runs as a Workato action or webhook" lowers adoption friction in that account. Build it later, behind the same adapter interface (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §9 already proposes a narrow API).
- **Do not write to the ERP's cost master automatically.** Standard-cost updates are a governed finance step (a revaluation guide separates "the decision to change a standard cost from the review and posting", [Hyperbots](https://www.hyperbots.com/glossary/dynamics-gp-standard-cost-revaluation), S). Emit a proposal plus its audit trail and let finance release it (Inf).
- **For the demo:** Bowman's PLM and ERP are stand-ins (`software-options.md`). Say plainly that real Teamcenter and SAP adapters are untested; I did not check Teamcenter or SAP API details.

**Positioning against "glue".**
- Glue moves a record from A to B unchanged. We produce a new judgement that exists in neither system: a range, its drivers, and the assumptions behind it.
- Suggested line: "Connectors tell SAP the change happened. We tell finance what it will cost before the board approves it." That fits Hyphenate's FP&A framing.
- Expect the judge question "won't Siemens or Palantir do this?" Honest answer: Siemens ships BOM impact inside Teamcenter now and says Intelligence Center X will span ERP data; Aras says cost and margin prediction is on its roadmap. We differ by being finance-first, ranged and auditable, and PLM-neutral. Don't claim the category is empty.
- Avoid: "integration platform", "connector", "sync". Use: "impact assessment", "change impact statement", "cost range with assumptions".

**Checks before relying on this**
1. Ask the Archer contacts how an ECR impact assessment is built today and who owns each number. That is the missing primary evidence.
2. Read the Teamcenter AI BOM agent docs (not the marketing blog) for any cost or supply-chain scope.
3. Check whether Archer's Palantir work already touches ECRs.
