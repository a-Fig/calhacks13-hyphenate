# Cal Hacks 13.0 logistics

Short summary. All times PT. Full text is in the linked sources.

## Venue and travel

- Palace of Fine Arts, 3601 Lyon St, San Francisco, CA 94123. Hacking in the main hall and the mezzanine ([hacker guide](../sources/calhacks/hacker-guide.md), [Pre Event #3 email](../sources/calhacks/emails/2026-10-22-pre-event-details-3.md)).
- Free buses from Montgomery BART on Friday and back on Sunday ([hacker guide](../sources/calhacks/hacker-guide.md)).
- Bring a government ID with DOB and the Hacker Pass from the H@B app. No personal hotspots ([Pre Event #3](../sources/calhacks/emails/2026-10-22-pre-event-details-3.md), [Opening Ceremony email](../sources/calhacks/emails/2026-10-23-opening-ceremony.md)).

## Deadlines and judging

- Submit on Devpost by Sun 09:00. Late projects are not judged, and you must be at the venue for judging ([hacker guide](../sources/calhacks/hacker-guide.md), [devpost-dates.md](../sources/calhacks/devpost-dates.md)).
- Judges visit tables 10:30-12:30. The whole team stays at the table for the full window ([hacker guide](../sources/calhacks/hacker-guide.md)).
- Pitch length differs by source. Hacker guide recommends two minutes ([hacker guide](../sources/calhacks/hacker-guide.md)), Devpost says three minutes plus two of questions ([devpost-overview.md](../sources/calhacks/devpost-overview.md)), the FAQ says 5 minutes or under ([faq.json](../sources/calhacks/faq.json)).
- Submission needs a 2-3 sentence elevator pitch, table number and team name ([faq.json](../sources/calhacks/faq.json)), plus an image and the GitHub repo ([devpost-overview.md](../sources/calhacks/devpost-overview.md)).

## Rules

- Teams of 1-4 per the hacker guide, "2 to 4 members" per Devpost ([hacker guide](../sources/calhacks/hacker-guide.md), [devpost-overview.md](../sources/calhacks/devpost-overview.md)). Ages 18+, college students only ([devpost-overview.md](../sources/calhacks/devpost-overview.md)).
- Ideas can come before, but all implementation happens during hacking. Prior work is disqualified ([faq.json](../sources/calhacks/faq.json)).
- Code of Conduct ([devpost-rules.md](../sources/calhacks/devpost-rules.md)).

## Judging criteria

Devpost: Application, Intentionality, Functionality, Creativity, Technical Complexity ([devpost-overview.md](../sources/calhacks/devpost-overview.md)). Hyphenate's own four questions are in [hyphenate-track.md](hyphenate-track.md).

Slack #ask-mentors and mentor tables, the concierge desk, and #0-ask-directors for rules questions ([hacker guide](../sources/calhacks/hacker-guide.md), [faq.json](../sources/calhacks/faq.json)).
