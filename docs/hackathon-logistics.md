## Deadlines and judging

- Submit on Devpost by Sun Oct 25, 09:00. Devpost calls it a hard deadline and the Hacker Guide says late projects are not judged.
- Shortly after 09:00 every project gets a table number. Be at that table by 10:30. Tables are shared by 2-3 teams.
- Judges visit tables 10:30-12:30. Stay for the whole window. If the entire team is not there when a judge comes, the project is not judged.
- A live demo is required (Hacker Guide). Devpost also asks for a video demo for sponsors judging asynchronously, and Hyphenate wants a demo video that opens with "the moment".
- Pitch length differs by source. Hacker Guide recommends a two-minute pitch. Devpost says three minutes to pitch and two minutes of questions. The live FAQ says presentations must be 5 minutes or under.

## Submission requirements

- Devpost: include an image of the project and the GitHub repository for any code. Add teammates on Devpost. You must be accepted and checked in to submit.
- Live FAQ: a 2-3 sentence elevator pitch, the team location (table number) and the team name.
- Hyphenate: demo video opening with the moment, live demo, one line on the seats the system fills, and proof it works (what's real, what's simulated, how we know).

## Rules

- Teams of 1-4. The Hacker Guide says "no more than 4 people allowed under any circumstance". Devpost's sidebar says "Team required: 2 to 4 members". Teams can change until submission.
- Ages 18+ and college students only (Devpost).
- All implementation must happen during the hacking period. Ideas can come before. Cal Hacks says it disqualifies entries for prior work every year.
- Follow the MLH Code of Conduct and the Cal Hacks Code of Conduct (https://hackberkeley.org/conduct.pdf).

## General judging criteria

Devpost: Application (real-world use, who it helps), Intentionality (purposeful design for its users), Functionality (live demo free of major bugs, plus a video demo), Creativity, Technical Complexity.

Hacker Guide and live FAQ: impact, functionality (or quality), technical complexity and creativity.

## Help

Slack #ask-mentors and the mentor tables across from the Robotics Space. Organizers at the concierge desk near the entrance or in white event shirts. Slack #ask-directors for rules questions.
