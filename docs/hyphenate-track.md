# Hyphenate Track

"The Hyphenated Enterprise: Autonomous Systems for Finance", Cal Hacks 13.0. Short summary. Full text is in the linked sources.

## Prizes

- $8,000 / $5,000 / $3,000 cash for the top 3 teams, expedited interview process for the top 5 teams ([prize page entry](../sources/hyphenate/track-brief.md#prize-page-entry), [Devpost prize list](../sources/calhacks/devpost-overview.md)).
- Prizes are per team, and every member of a top-5 team gets an expedited, shortened interview for internships and full-time roles ([Meet Hyphenate email](../sources/calhacks/emails/2026-10-16-meet-hyphenate.md)).

## How it's judged

Four questions, the same for every submission ([track brief](../sources/hyphenate/track-brief.md)).

1. Is it real enough? Realistic data, a messy problem, proof it works.
2. Would you hire it? Named seats, limited access, a clean record of its work, knowing when to ask.
3. Did we see something new? A company, problem or approach not made autonomous before.
4. The moment. It catches what others missed, recovers from an error, or defends a decision.

Cal Hacks' general criteria also apply: Application, Intentionality, Functionality, Creativity, Technical Complexity ([devpost-overview.md](../sources/calhacks/devpost-overview.md)).

## What to bring

From the [track brief](../sources/hyphenate/track-brief.md#linked-brief-doc-calhacks_hyphenate_track).

- A demo video that opens with the moment
- A live demo at judging, running for real (they may hand us their own scenario)
- One line on which finance seats the system fills
- Proof it works: what's real, what's simulated, and how we know (test set, planted errors with an answer key, or a benchmark)

## What makes it hard

Sources that disagree, time (March changes June), working with approvers and colleagues, limited access and segregation of duties, and decisions that stay explainable later ([track brief, "What makes it hard"](../sources/hyphenate/track-brief.md)). The brief also lists sample workflows (revenue, AR, treasury, AP, accruals, close, FP&A, reporting), starter sandboxes, ledgers, mocks, datasets and benchmarks, and key finance concepts.
